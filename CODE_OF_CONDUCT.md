# Edge-AI Digital Twin Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free, and academically honest project environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept technical feedback professionally.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its learning objectives.
- Credit other people's work, data, and ideas appropriately.
- Report limitations, failures, and experimental results honestly.
- Raise electrical, mechanical, data, or security risks promptly.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help correct them.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive conduct
- Trolling, insulting comments, sexualised language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Fabricating results, hiding known failures, falsifying contribution history, or presenting others' work as one's own
- Pressuring a contributor to bypass a safety control, validation step, or responsible disclosure process
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## Engineering and Academic Integrity

Project evidence must distinguish between **planned**, **implemented**, and **validated** work. Contributors must not invent accuracy, latency, sensor behaviour, test results, literature claims, or individual contributions. Controlled experiments must follow the safety plan and remain within approved component ratings.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, experiments, demonstrations, reports, presentations, and other interactions made on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer using the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/krutharth-dev). Do not post sensitive details publicly.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct violates this policy.
